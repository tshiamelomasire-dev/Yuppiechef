About Yuppiechef

Our story, mission, and values.

Our Story

Yuppiechef was founded in 2006 by Andrew Smith and Shane Dryden as a part-time side hustle from a lounge in Plumstead, Cape Town. What started with just 32 products has grown into South Africa's premier online retailer of quality kitchen and homeware.

In 2021, Yuppiechef was acquired by Mr Price Group for approximately R470 million. Today, we operate 25 physical stores nationwide and offer over 12,000 products.

Our Mission

To bring the world's best kitchen and homeware to South Africans, making cooking and home living more enjoyable.

Our Vision

To be the destination of choice for anyone seeking to elevate their home and culinary experiences.
